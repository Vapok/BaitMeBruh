# 1.0.0 - Initial Release
* **Early Rod Progression**: Craft the new Primitive Fishing Rod at a level 1 Workbench using wood, leather scraps, and bone fragments. Includes a custom 3D model and inventory icon.
* **Dynamic Line Tension**: Replaced vanilla flat stamina drain with a line tension system. Fish cycle between struggling and resting; reel during rest to pull fish in, and ease off during struggles to avoid breaking the line.
* **Casting & Distance HUD**: Added a real-time casting gauge with distance readout, comfortable 1-2 second safe draw timing, tap casting (3-5m), and overcast warnings if held beyond rod limits.
* **Quick Bait Switching**: Cycle through baits in your inventory while holding any fishing rod before casting by pressing **G** on keyboard (hold **Shift** to reverse) or **Right Bumper** (RB/R1) on gamepad.
* **Context-Sensitive KeyHints**: In-game key hints update dynamically across each fishing phase (Pull, Strike, Reel, Release, and Switch Bait) for both mouse/keyboard and controller players.
* **Sensory Bite Cues**: Added audio cues, water splash effects, a warm light cue at the bobber, and an on-screen strike banner when a fish bites.
* **Seated Angling & Boat Trolling**: Fish while seated aboard boats, benches, or rudders. Moving vessels dynamically spool line behind the boat instead of immediately snapping.
* **Autonomous Harvesting**: Submerge Bait Creels in shallow water to turn Neck Tails into fishing bait over time. Deploy portable Coastal and Deep-Sea Fish Nets from land or boats to passively catch regional fish.
* **Biome Bait Crafting**: Added 9 crafting recipes at the Workbench and Cauldron to craft all biome fishing baits using organic regional materials without merchant or trophy restrictions.
* **Campfire & Cauldron Cooking**: Roast whole Perch and Pike directly over campfires. Brew Trollfish Chowder with yellow mushrooms at the Cauldron for a 20-minute stamina meal and +10 Sneak skill bonus, featuring a custom 3D serving bowl.
* **Multi-Language Support**: Complete native translations across 34 official languages supported by Valheim.
* **Fully Configurable**: All crafting recipes, food values, trap intervals, and HUD positions can be customized through the configuration file or in-game mod menu.
* **Dependencies**: Built against Jotunn 2.30.2 and internal dependencies for stability.
